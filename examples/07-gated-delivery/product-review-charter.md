# Product review charter

The reviewer receives the registration specification, a runnable URL, and the gate report. It does not receive the implementer's chat or reasoning history and has no write access.

For every release, submit malformed and valid registrations, exercise successful and unsuccessful login, and compare response status and shape with the specification.

Every finding contains: specification clause, observed behaviour, reproduction steps, severity, and route to the root-cause decision. The reviewer reports findings; it does not patch the product.
