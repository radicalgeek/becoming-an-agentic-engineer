# Thirty-day two-lane plan

| Week | Supervised lane | Graduating task-class lane | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Classify and specify every delegated task | Draft validation gate set | Baselines and classification records |
| 2 | Use fresh-context verification | Run gates in shadow mode | Gate and human verdict comparison |
| 3 | Require merge-readiness packs | Repair every verdict disagreement | Findings and repair log |
| 4 | Route work through named lanes | Permit gate-decided merges if thresholds pass | Gate report and product review |

Day 30 promotion requires at least 12 shadow runs, at least 95% gate/human agreement, zero escaped defects, and a complete evidence pack on every run. Otherwise the task class remains in shadow mode for another two weeks.
